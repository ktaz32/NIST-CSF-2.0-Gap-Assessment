# Assessment Scope

## Framework

This assessment uses the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.

The assessment considers the six CSF Functions:

- GOVERN
- IDENTIFY
- PROTECT
- DETECT
- RESPOND
- RECOVER

---

## Objective

The objective is to identify material cybersecurity capability gaps, evaluate their maturity, translate significant gaps into business risks, and recommend prioritized remediation actions.

---

## In-Scope Areas

The assessment covers:

### Governance
- cybersecurity governance
- roles and responsibilities
- cybersecurity risk management
- third-party cybersecurity risk

### Asset and Risk Management
- asset inventory
- risk identification
- vulnerability management
- threat awareness

### Identity and Access
- authentication
- privileged access
- access reviews
- cloud IAM

### Data and Platform Protection
- endpoint protection
- secure configuration
- vulnerability remediation
- data protection

### Detection
- security logging
- monitoring coverage
- detection engineering
- alert analysis

### Incident Response
- incident preparation
- triage
- containment
- communication
- lessons learned

### Recovery
- recovery planning
- backups
- recovery testing
- post-incident restoration

---

## Out of Scope

The following are outside the scope of this portfolio assessment:

- physical penetration testing
- production application penetration testing
- legal compliance certification
- formal ISO 27001 certification
- financial audit
- privacy impact assessment
- production customer environments
- real employee data

---

## Assessment Method

Each selected NIST CSF 2.0 outcome will be evaluated using:

1. Defined current-state evidence
2. Observed control implementation
3. Documented scoring rationale
4. Current maturity score
5. Target maturity score
6. Identified gap
7. Risk implication
8. Recommended remediation
9. Remediation priority

---

## Evidence Principle

Maturity scores are assigned only after the supporting evidence and scoring rationale have been documented.

The assessment follows:

```text
Evidence
    ↓
Observed Control State
    ↓
Scoring Rationale
    ↓
Maturity Score
```

Scores must not be selected first and justified retrospectively.

---

## Evidence Sources

Evidence may include:

- fictional policies and procedures defined for the case study
- modeled technical control states
- cloud-security findings
- detection-engineering artifacts
- SOC investigation capabilities
- documented control-process observations

Where relevant, technical portfolio projects may be referenced as examples of control validation capability.

---

## Assessment Boundary

The assessment represents a point-in-time review of the fictional Northstar HealthTech environment.

It does not constitute certification, assurance, or attestation against NIST CSF 2.0.
